# Minimum Buffer Stock SOP
## Government District Hospital Supply-Chain Standard Operating Procedure
### Document: SOP-SC-001 | Version: 3.2 | Effective: 2026-01-01

---

## 1. Purpose

This SOP establishes the minimum buffer stock levels for essential medicines
and medical supplies at all government district hospitals to prevent stock-outs
and ensure uninterrupted patient care.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to all Class A (critical) and Class B (essential) items on the
National Essential Medicines List (NEML) maintained at district and
sub-district government hospitals.

---

## 3. Minimum Buffer Stock Requirements

All facilities must maintain a minimum buffer stock equal to 14 days of
average consumption for Class A items (insulin, oxygen, IV fluids,
surgical gloves, blood products) and 7 days for Class B items
(antibiotics, analgesics, antidiabetics, antihypertensives).

A stock level below the 14-day buffer for Class A items must be immediately
reported to the District Supply Chain Manager within 24 hours.

---

## 4. Reorder Triggers

- **Class A items**: Place reorder when stock falls to 21 days of supply.
- **Class B items**: Place reorder when stock falls to 14 days of supply.
- Emergency procurement may be initiated when stock falls below the minimum buffer.

---

## 5. Supplier Delay Protocol

If a confirmed supplier delay exceeds 5 days for any Class A item:
1. Immediately notify the District Medical Officer.
2. Initiate inter-facility transfer request from the nearest facility with surplus.
3. Activate the Emergency Procurement Procedure (EPP-SC-003).
4. Document the disruption in the Supply Chain Event Log within 48 hours.

If the delay exceeds 10 days, escalate to the State Health Secretary.

---

## 6. Expiry Management (FEFO Policy)

All facilities must follow First Expiry First Out (FEFO) dispensing.
Stock expiring within 30 days must be flagged in the inventory system.
Stock expiring within 7 days that cannot be consumed or transferred must
be reported for destruction per the Waste Disposal SOP (WD-001).

Inter-facility transfer is encouraged for stock expiring within 30 days
if the originating facility's consumption rate is insufficient to use
the stock before expiry.

---

## 7. Inter-Facility Transfer

Transfers between facilities within a district are permitted and encouraged
to prevent both stock-outs and expiry wastage. Approval is required from
both facility in-charges. Transfer documentation must be completed within
24 hours of physical transfer. The District Supply Chain dashboard must be
updated on the same day.

Maximum transfer quantity: 50% of the donor facility's current stock
of the item, ensuring the donor facility retains at least a 14-day buffer.
