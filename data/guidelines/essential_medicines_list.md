## Critical Dependency Notes

- **Insulin**: Required by all diabetic patients, ICU patients on glucose drips,
  and post-surgical patients. Departments affected: Endocrinology, Medicine Ward,
  ICU, Surgical Ward, Emergency.

- **IV Saline / IV Fluids**: Required for all surgical procedures, IV drug
  administration, and dehydration management. Affects all wards.

- **Surgical Gloves**: Required for all clinical procedures, surgeries, and
  emergency interventions. Affects all departments.

- **Paracetamol**: First-line antipyretic/analgesic. Affects all wards,
  outpatient departments, and pediatric care.

- **Amoxicillin**: First-line antibiotic for respiratory, urinary, and skin
  infections. Affects Medicine, Pediatrics, Surgical, OPD.
