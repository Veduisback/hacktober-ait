# Inter-Facility Transfer Policy
## District Health Authority – Supply Chain Transfer Procedures
### Document: IFT-SC-002 | Version: 2.1 | Effective: 2026-01-01

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs the transfer of medicines and medical supplies between
government health facilities within a district to optimize utilization,
prevent stock-outs, and minimize expiry wastage.

---

## 2. Eligibility for Transfer

A facility may request an inter-facility transfer when:
- Current stock is below the minimum buffer level (per SOP-SC-001).
- A confirmed supplier delay creates a projected stock-out risk.
- Urgency is classified as HIGH or MEDIUM by the Supply Chain AI system
  or the facility pharmacist.

A facility may donate stock when:
- It holds more than 150% of its 30-day requirement.
- The donated quantity will not reduce the donor's stock below its 14-day buffer.
- The stock has an expiry date more than 30 days away OR the receiving facility
  can consume it before expiry (FEFO exception transfer).

---

## 3. Transfer Approval

| Quantity | Approval Authority |
|---|---|
| Up to 20% of item stock | Facility Pharmacist (self-approved) |
| 21–50% of item stock | District Supply Chain Manager |
| More than 50% of item stock | District Medical Officer + State Depot confirmation |

Emergency transfers for Class A items may be verbally approved and
documented retrospectively within 24 hours.

---

## 4. Transfer Process

1. **Initiating facility** logs a Transfer Request in the Supply Chain system.
2. **Donor facility** confirms available quantity and expiry dates.
3. **District SCM** approves (or auto-approves if under threshold).
4. Physical transfer with accompanying Transfer Voucher (Form IFT-01).
5. Both facilities update inventory within 24 hours of physical transfer.
6. District dashboard is updated automatically on inventory update.

---

## 5. Priority Transfer Items

The following items are eligible for expedited (same-day) transfer approval:
- All Class A items (see NEML-2025)
- Any item with a confirmed stock-out risk within 3 days
- Any item with critical expiry risk (expiring within 7 days)

---

## 6. Cost and Logistics

Intra-district transfers use government vehicle allocation.
Cost of transport is borne by the District Health Authority.
Facilities must not refuse a district-sanctioned transfer request
without written justification to the District Medical Officer.

---

## 7. AI-Assisted Transfer Recommendations

The District Supply Chain AI system may automatically generate transfer
recommendations based on real-time stock levels, consumption forecasts,
and supplier delay alerts. These recommendations are advisory only and
must be reviewed and approved by a qualified supply-chain officer before
execution. AI recommendations must be shown with a confidence score
and the source data used in the analysis.
